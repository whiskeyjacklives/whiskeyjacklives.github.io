# Privacy Policy — Typhon Backup

Typhon Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- **What it accesses:** only its owner's Google Drive, to upload, read, verify and delete encrypted backup files.
- **Why:** backup, restore, integrity checks and retention of the owner's own data.
- **What it collects:** no data from anyone other than the owner.
- **Sharing:** nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Google provides the storage.
- **Credentials:** stored only on the owner's own devices.
- **Revoking access:** at any time in Google Account settings → Security → Third-party connections. Revoking access does not delete stored backups.
